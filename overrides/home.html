// WHAT EVERY CHART GETS

One reviewed manifest in, a verified, scanned and signed chart and image set out.

Vendored verbatim

The pinned upstream chart is committed byte-for-byte under vendor/, and its helm template output under rendered/ — so a bump's diff is the change.

Digest-pinned images

Every image the rendered chart references — plus the ones helm can't see — is resolved to a digest in images.lock.yaml and mirrored under the platform registry's images/.

Upstream provenance

Charts and images are verified against the publisher's cosign identity or key before mirroring. Unsigned upstreams are declared as provider: none — a documented gap, never a silent one.

Scan gates that expire

grype blocks CRITICAL and HIGH findings with a fix upstream. Accepted findings need a statement and an expired_at within 90 days, and a weekly rescan catches CVEs published after merge.

Keyless signing

The publish workflow signs every chart and image with its GitHub OIDC identity. Clusters pin that one identity — no KMS key, nothing secret on the registry path.

Lockstep updates

A daily sweep opens one PR per update group. Charts released as a pair — the Flux operator and instance, ARC's controller and scale set — always bump together.
